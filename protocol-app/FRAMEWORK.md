## The thesis

**The unit of this app is the day, not the item.** The app composes the day; the person doesn't pick from a list. Two missions that resolve into one design:

1. **Structure removes the scheduling decisions.** Nobody chooses between doing everything, doing nothing, or puzzling out what-when-how. The blueprint is handed over.
2. **Content trains the sensing.** The deepest skill taught is listening to the body — noticing what's tight, weak, or offline. Years of survival mode taught people to ignore those signals; the app rebuilds the habit of hearing them.

What begins as concerted, conscious effort becomes baseline. The app's success is measured by how much of itself becomes unnecessary. (Claim held at the level of movement patterns and posture; deeper autonomic restoration is the working hypothesis, graded honestly in the evidence review.)

## The day arc

Six parts. Morning and evening are each **two-part anchors** — miss one part and you've still done something.

**1. Wake block — in bed, before feet touch the floor.** 2–5 minutes. Rocking child's pose, lean-forward lat and shoulder stretch, kneel-sit chest opener. Content follows the **unwind-the-night principle**: the morning bias reverses whatever position the body held for hours. Set by a sleep-position question at onboarding (mixed/unknown option routes to taught self-detection, D30), refined over time. *Floor version: sixty seconds of rocking.*

**2. Rise block — woven into the ritual you already have.** Movement attaches to an existing ritual (coffee brewing, kettle, shower warming): forward fold, hip circles, horse stance with pelvic rocks and weight shifts. The anchor ritual itself belongs to the user; the app schedules around it, never inside it.

**3. Session 1 — the main block, early by default.** Release and strengthen per the composer (pairing law governs), then breathwork part 1. Scales with the dial. Movable for real schedules; the app is honest about why early sticks.

**4. Through the day — three layers.**
- *Awareness layer (trained, not scheduled):* the skill of noticing and returning — stack up, reverse the joint, squeeze and hold, belly button to spine. Delivered through connection cards and notice-lines, not compliance pings.
- *Movement snacks (scheduled, swappable):* CARs, hip planes, arm circles, toe touches, squats, twists — bite-size doses biased toward deep core, hips, spine, shoulders, chest opening, pelvic floor, diaphragm. Every snack belongs to a swap group with alternatives across contexts.
- *Ambient practices (opportunistic):* TV-time single-leg balance and proprioception (near a counter, per the POTS note); seated twists and chest openers; ball-on-foot fascia work; acupressure mat as an afternoon restorative.

**5. Evening block, part 1 — deep release.** The primary offenders get deep release; the melt-into-the-floor response is the body's signal that it's safe to downshift. Breathing through intensity is itself taught. Order: release, then breath (F1, settled).

**6. Evening block, part 2 — in-bed wind-down.** Supine knee rocks; prone bent-knee foot swings — rhythmic motions that carry people into sleep. *Floor version: one minute of knee rocks.*

## The laws — rules the composer obeys

1. **Pairing law (hard).** Release is never scheduled alone; the session that opens a region ends by loading it, and the next 2–3 days echo activation in the new range.
2. **Reciprocal pairing.** Prefer movements that strengthen one muscle while stretching its partner.
3. **Coverage law.** Regions and muscles rotate by recency and weight; the promise is everything touched within its window, not everything daily.
4. **Rhythm before variety.** Anchors stay stable; variety lives in the main block and snacks.
5. **Self-healing, not debt.** Missed days create no backlog and no guilt; rotation resumes where coverage is thinnest.
6. **Every anchor has a floor.** Each block has a ~60-second minimum; missing part one never forfeits part two.
7. **Attach to existing rituals.** New habits hang on old ones; the app colonizes dead time.
8. **Listen and lean.** The body's feedback picks the emphasis within a slot; the app teaches the finding.
9. **Medicine drop.** Periodically the composer deals one avoided-but-needed item; one honest tap skips it, and it returns. (~1–2×/week, F4 default.)
10. **Capacity gates.** The PEM/capacity gate sets the movement dial's start and caps its ramp (education per D28 — self-designation, never screening-as-diagnosis). Sharp pain, joint giving-way, lightheadedness are stop-and-evaluate signals, never push-through moments. The D42 *pain or joint* chip routes here.
11. **Nobody's case earns schema.** Schema exists only for patterns many kinds of content share. Personal protocols — including the founder's — are content and settings. Supplements are ordinary items with an optional time-or-meal anchor and a check-off.

## The dial and per-item targets

- **Time:** Light (~5 min per anchor + snacks) · Standard (~15) · Deep (~30+). The composer fills slots to budget.
- **Intensity:** gentle through hardcore, per person, within each item's range; capacity gates can cap it.
- **Weekly targets are user-set per item** (K1): content ships with a suggested default, the user picks their number, the rotation deals exactly that many; anything beyond is theirs to freelance.

## The composer, the ledger, and findings

**One runtime composer — two inputs (D16 as amended, Kevin Aug 21).** v1 composer scope: **arithmetic, not AI.** Day templates per dial, weighted rotation, swap groups, and the laws as hard constraints. There is no second composition engine anywhere in the system.

- **Inputs, side one — presets:** quiz output and templates (K5) are the same mechanism: module toggles + seeded findings/weights + dial and safety settings over the one shared catalog. The quiz's composed-start reveal ("why you're seeing this, in your own words") is a presentation of provenance, not a separate engine.
- **Inputs, side two — the living state:** coverage ledger, findings + weights, per-item targets, context profile, capacity gates.
- **Coverage ledger.** The app counts, per muscle and per role — released, strengthened, stretched, balanced — by day and by week (vocabulary per D40: data-not-code, introduced-on semantics). The composer reads the ledger before dealing a day. A swap debits the same account.
- **Relationship to protocols:** anchored, scheduled protocol items (D15 machinery) are standing appointments; the composer deals the rotating day-arc content around them onto the same Today timeline. Honest residue, carried from the ruling: the v1 composer does not read anchored load when filling its budget; if trial use surfaces stacking, the fix is one more composer input — never a second engine.
- **Findings layer (D42 model — supersedes v2's two-tap "too hard"/"felt weak" model in full).** Seeds come from the quiz; the deeper source is discovery during sessions:
  - ***Hot spot*** and ***eased up*** taps stand unchanged: hot spot raises a muscle's weight; eased up decays it toward baseline so last month's discovery doesn't rule forever.
  - **Post-item rating, optional, 1–5.** Check-off alone is always complete. A 4–5 asks one follow-up naming the limiting factor: ***muscle gave out*** → finding, weight up, paired strengthening rides along per law 1 · ***ran out of steam*** → system limit, timestamps exertion for trends and the 48-hour look-back, no muscle arithmetic · ***pain or joint*** → law-10 stop-and-evaluate card, no arithmetic. Skipped follow-up recorded without arithmetic; the app may ask once more at the item's next deal.
  - **Tap arithmetic is uniform for everyone.** The pacing profile governs dial defaults and copy, never tap math.
  - **Difficulty ≠ helpfulness.** The not-helpful signal lives on the same optional sheet (see Ratings) and only ever means "deal this less, promote its siblings" — it never touches muscle weights.

## The content system

**Content tiers.** *Established* (the basics) and *Exploratory* — clearly labeled "worth trying," promoted or retired by user feedback. This is how new ideas enter a health app without inflating claims.

**Catalog as data.** Items live as data files; hundreds of exercises get added over time without code changes, and feedback ranks what gets built next. The v1 seed is sized so every swap group has a real alternative in every common context. The muscle vocabulary is part of the catalog data (D40).

## Regions and notable content

Priority regions: feet/ankles · calves · hamstrings · glutes/hips · deep core/pelvic floor · lower back/spine · **neck/jaw** · shoulders · chest/ribs.

- **Neck, first-class.** The heavy-head pattern: forward-hanging head multiplies its own load. Two-sided fix — stacking awareness plus deep-neck-flexor activation and gentle isometrics. Careful field: hypermobile necks get gentle dosing, no forced end-range, route-to-clinician language.
- **Hands, feet, jaw, forearms = entry content.** Dense with findable points, self-treatable at a desk, instant feedback. The thenar-web point gives nearly everyone a guaranteed find inside minute one; the first-run experience is designed around it.
- **Tender-point & release map (exploratory tier).** What's anatomically there, how to press, what ease-under-pressure means. Traditional names and claims noted *as claims*. Teaches **referral**: where it hurts is often not where it's from. Reverse referral lookup (D37) grows over it point-by-point, careful-field gated.
- **Joint-security block (D39):** recognize / respond / prevent, law-4 framing, evidence gate before ship.

## The awareness curriculum

Teaching model: **tactical mindset** — explicit heuristics drilled until habitual. Small **connection cards**, dripped one at a time. Reinforced by notice-lines on ordinary cards. The loop closes: **every one-tap finding is a rep of the noticing skill** — the mechanism that makes the composer smarter and the mission of the app are the same feature. ## Ratings and feedback

- The "helpful?" signal lives on the **optional post-item sheet** (with the D42 rating), off the card face. Locally: down-voted items dealt less; swap-group siblings promoted. Never touches muscle weights.
- **Segmentation rule:** a rating only means something within context — template plus findings flags — never global karma.
